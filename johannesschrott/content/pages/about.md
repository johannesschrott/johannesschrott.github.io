Hi!

I am Johannes Schrott, a computer science PhD student at the
<a href="https://dmki-tuwien.github.io">Data Management and Knowledge-Driven AI Lab</a>
at the
<a href="https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/people/johannes-schrott">Faculty of Informatics</a>
at
<a href="https://tuwien.ac.at">TU Wien</a>.

My research interests include data management, knowledge graphs, data quality, and everything in-between these fields.
For details see my [publications](#publications), [datasets](#datasets) and [tools](#tools) listed below.
