      Hi!

      I am Johannes Schrott, a computer science PhD student at the Data Management and Knowledge-Driven AI Lab at the Faculty of Informatics at TU Wien.

      My research interests include data management, knowledge graphs, data quality, and everything in-between these fields. For details see my publications, datasets and tools listed below.
